Test case Design

to write the test cases, we need to do
	.understand the requirements
		user stories/ epic (JIRA)
		discuss within the team and if any clarifications needed, get answers from product owner
	.identify the scenarios
		positive and negative scenarios
	.design the scenarios
Note: 1 scenario will have one or more testcases

to write the testcases, we have to follow the testcase template
   	. TestcaseID
	. ScenarioID
	. TestName
	. Pre conditions
	. Priority
	. Steps to run (how to test)
	. Expected Result
	. TestData
	. Actual results
	. Status
after test execution, if the actual results and expected result is same, then the status is passed. otherwise status is failed 

Terms used:   Mobile- screen   web- page   desktop- window
example: for desktop,    Verify the login page
